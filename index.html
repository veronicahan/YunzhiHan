Hi, I’m Yunzhi Han (韩昀芝), and you can also call me Vera. I study fine-grained phonetic variation, with a particular interest in the acoustic properties of second-language (L2) speech. I also teach Mandarin Chinese to speakers of other languages. Outside of research and teaching, I enjoy resistance training and cooking.
